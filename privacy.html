Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information JustFlip does not collect, what limited technical data may be processed by third-party services, and how privacy is handled across the app.

Last updated June 2026

Daniel Čech ("we", "us", or "our") operates the JustFlip mobile application (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our Application.

1. Personal Data We Do Not Collect

1.1. We do not require account registration.

1.2. We do not collect, store, or have access to your name, email address, password, or any personal identifiers.

1.3. Your app data (flashcards, images) is stored on your device and in your personal Apple iCloud storage. We cannot access this data.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Collection

To improve the performance and reliability of the Application, we use third-party analytics and monitoring tools. These tools may collect certain non-personal, technical information automatically:

2.1. Google Analytics for Firebase
We use this service to understand how users interact with the Application (e.g., which features are used most frequently). This data is anonymized and aggregated.

2.2. Firebase Crashlytics
We use this service to monitor app stability and collect crash reports. If the Application crashes, Crashlytics collects technical details about the crash (e.g., device type, operating system version, and the state of the app at the time of the crash) to help us fix the issue.

3. Children's Privacy

3.1. Since we do not collect any personal data, our Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

4.1. We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the Application or on our website.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: daniel.cech@gmail.com.
