The science of modern learning

Classic studying is a myth.
Learn what actually works.

Remember the sleepless nights spent highlighting textbooks, or reading the same notes over and over? Where this comes from

In the landmark book Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning, researchers showed that our intuition often betrays us when we study. This is the story of why we learn the wrong way — and how JustFlip! lets you change it today.

01

A trap called the illusion of knowing.

The greatest enemy of effective learning is your own brain. Read a text three or four times in a row and it starts to feel like familiar ground. The information seems known — and you convince yourself you've mastered it.

Experts call this the illusion of knowing. We are simply poor judges of how much we've actually learned. The real test comes only at the moment we have to pull the information out of our head without a hint. And that's exactly where we so often fail.

02

Self-testing: the most honest mirror of your memory.

So how do you escape the trap? The authors of Make It Stick offer an unambiguous answer: the best tool for learning is self-testing.

When you actively try to recall an answer to a question, you force your brain to work. It isn't just a knowledge check — the very act of retrieving a memory changes the structure of your brain and strengthens neural connections. Self-testing is the most honest method there is: it exposes your gaps immediately.

For it to work perfectly, two key principles need to join in. Together they are known as spaced repetition.

Timed retrieval

Review just as you begin to forget

It sounds paradoxical, but memory is strengthened most when you recall a fact at the exact moment it starts slipping away. That desirable effort is what writes knowledge into long-term memory.

Interleaving

Mix your topics

Instead of drilling one narrow topic for an hour, it's far better to interleave subjects and areas. Your brain has to keep switching and re-establishing context — which leads to results that last.

Let's be honest

Learning that works hurts a little

We won't promise miracles. JustFlip! is no magic wand that pours knowledge into your head without work — proper learning takes effort. It's completely normal to feel a bit frustrated at first, mildly “confused”, or like you're going slowly. That is not a sign of failure — it's the moment your brain is genuinely rewiring. Forgetting is natural and can't be stopped, but with these methods you'll keep many times more in memory. You'll see the real difference within a few days.
