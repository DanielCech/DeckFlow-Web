03

Let the AI write the deck.

Install the JustFlip! skill into your favorite agentic tool — Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode — and ask for the cards you need. The agent drafts, structures and exports a .flashcard archive that JustFlip! imports in a single drag. No manual typing, no copy-paste, no broken formatting.

justflip ~ claude-code 
~ claude > Make me a deck of 30 Spanish irregular verbs in present tense, with example sentences.

▸ skill justflip-cards
▸ model claude-opus-4-7
▸ output spanish-irregulars.flashcard.zip

✓ Drafted 30 cards across 3 interests
✓ Wrote markdown for fronts & backs
✓ Bundled audio cues and resources
✓ Exported spanish-irregulars.flashcard.zip

~ Drop the file onto JustFlip! on your Mac.▍

05

A patient algorithm.
Five states. Infinite memory.

JustFlip! runs a refined SM-2 engine across five learning states. Cards travel through unseen, learning, reviewing, relearning and graduated, each with its own interval and ease. Urgency tells you what is most worth your time today.

  1. UnseenNever studied — ready for first contact.
  2. LearningMinutes to a day. Fresh memory taking shape.
  3. ReviewingDays to weeks. The interval grows by ease.
  4. RelearningForgotten? Back to short intervals, gently.
  5. GraduatedWeeks to months. Long-term retention.
intervaln+1 = intervaln × easeFactor

Ease grows on easy, settles on good, and contracts on hard or again — never below 1.3.
