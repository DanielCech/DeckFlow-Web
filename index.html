05

A patient algorithm.
Five states. Lasting recall.

JustFlip! runs a refined SM-2 engine across five learning states. Cards travel through unseen, learning, reviewing, relearning and graduated, each with its own interval and ease. Urgency tells you what is most worth your time today.

  1. UnseenNever studied — ready for first contact.
  2. LearningMinutes to a day. Fresh memory taking shape.
  3. ReviewingDays to weeks. The interval grows by ease.
  4. RelearningForgotten? Back to short intervals, gently.
  5. GraduatedWeeks to months. Long-term retention.
intervaln+1 = intervaln × easeFactor

Ease grows on easy, settles on good, and contracts on hard or again — never below 1.3.

06

Not just flipping cards.
A proven way to remember.

Decades of memory research — popularized in the bestselling book Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning — point to one conclusion: we remember by retrieving, not rereading, and by spacing practice over time. JustFlip! turns that science into a daily habit.

Retrieval practice

Recalling beats rereading

Every card asks you to pull the answer from memory before you flip — that effort is what makes it stick.

Spaced practice

Let a little forgetting work for you

Reviews return just as memory starts to fade, when each repetition does the most good — the SM-2 engine's quiet job.

Interleaving

Mix it up

Shuffling topics in one session feels harder — and builds memory that lasts far longer than studying in blocks.

Desirable difficulty

A worthwhile struggle

Judging each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy keeps reviews honestly challenging — the kind of effort that deepens learning.

“Learning is deeper and more durable when it's effortful.”

— Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning
