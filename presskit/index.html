02

A calmer way to remember.

JustFlip! was created as an answer to two frustrations with study apps: the slow creep of paywalls in products people already rely on, and the dated interface of traditional Anki. It brings serious spaced-repetition algorithms — including SM-2 and FSRS — into a native Apple experience.

There is no account to create, no advertising and no tracking. Your decks stay in your personal iCloud, with fast CloudKit sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. It is the power of deliberate learning, presented with less friction.
